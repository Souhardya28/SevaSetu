# Demo walkthrough

Start: `npx expo start`, press `w`. Sign-in screen has one-tap demo accounts. Use **Profile > Reset all demo data** to start clean.

## Journey A: person-to-person seva (about 4 minutes)
1. Sign in as **Meera**. Home > *Request support*.
2. Tap the mic, then tap again to stop. A demo transcript fills the box (no real microphone). Press *Structure my request*.
3. Check step 2: your original words stay visible above the AI structure. Tap *Edit the structure* to change anything. Continue.
4. Choose *Trusted campus or community circle*. Review the safety step (low risk). *Publish request*.
5. Tap *Continue as Aarav*. The request shows "Recommended because you selected mathematics, speak English and are available on Saturday."
6. *Accept this request*. Take the optional pause (or skip). *Yes, I will be there*.
7. In the room type "I want to help this poor woman" and send. A tone note appears; *Use suggestion*, then send.
8. *Check in*, then *Check out* (the other person checks in/out automatically, labelled demo). *Complete this session*.
9. Write a reflection, tap *Listening*, *Save*. See the related Abhiyan suggestion, then **Open Seva Journal**.
10. Optional: Profile > switch to Meera, open the completed room, give private feedback and an optional Prasad; switch back to Aarav and open *Prasad*.

## Journey B: transparent campaign (about 3 minutes)
1. Abhiyan > *Evening Learning Lamps*. Read the organizer dossier, roles, itemised budget.
2. *View all entries and add a bill*. Note the seeded "Possible duplicate" entry.
3. *Add or check a bill* > *Use sample bill A*. Watch the six steps. Read the findings (flag, not accusation). *Send for human review*.
4. *Continue as moderator*. Open the bill item: why flagged, confidence, evidence, recommendation. *Mark corrected* (or *Temporarily pause* to see status history on the campaign).
5. Back as Aarav: Abhiyan > campaign > the entry shows its correction note and the audit trail. Join *Teaching volunteer*: it asks for the safeguarding orientation first, then organizer approval.
6. Try *Use sample bill B* for the amount-mismatch case.

## Journey C: everyday Daan (about 2 minutes)
1. Daan > *Share* > sample photo *Class 10 books*. AI suggests *School books*; correct condition (try *Worn but usable*).
2. *See nearby needs*: a verified student and a library appear with distances. Continue; confirm the safety declaration; *Share this item*.
3. *Choose a nearby need*, pick one, choose a pickup point, *Reserve and create handover code*.
4. Act as receiver: confirm condition, add an optional thank-you, *Confirm receipt*. *Close listing*: the code expires.
5. Try typing "cooked food" or "medicine" as a description: the listing is blocked with a report-for-review option.

## Journey D: Seva Guide (about 2 minutes)
1. Tap the floating **Guide** button. Pick *Support* and say "I am feeling unmotivated".
2. The guide acknowledges, asks what support you want. Choose *Share a teaching*: a source-labelled passage (marked demo and unverified).
3. Choose *Suggest a small step* to open a low-pressure Abhiyan.
4. Crisis test: type "I want to end my life". The reply switches to safety routing with helplines and no philosophy. Nothing is sent to anyone.
5. *Learn* mode with "quantum lattice": "no verified passage found, no quotation shown".

## Extras
Profile: Hindi toggle, High contrast, Reduce motion, Simulate offline (banner, queued uploads, saved drafts). Privacy: consents, data export preview, deletion request. Safety: helplines, report, block, trusted contact, Code of Conduct.
